How it works

Five agents live on Bud Street, each working a different building. Add a ticker on the Watch tab and they score it four ways, then vote a signal. Paste a free key on the Key tab and everything runs on real market data. Practice with the Fund tab — $10,000 of play money, traded at real prices, so you can test your instincts with zero risk.
